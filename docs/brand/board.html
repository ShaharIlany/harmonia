The brief

Team Chat, renamed Harmonia: a Mac app where you work with six Claude Code teammates in channels, like a group chat that gets things done, with Mozart picking who answers. Harmonia is the Greek goddess of harmony and the everyday Hebrew word for it; the word first meant fitting things together, then tuning a lyre's strings to each other. The mark has to work as the macOS app icon from 16 to 1024 px, in the Dock, as the favicon, in the sidebar header and in the README, on light and dark, and stay clear of the speech bubbles, sparkles and node graphs every AI tool uses.

Final design

Lyre

Harmonia first meant tuning a lyre's strings to each other: six strings, one per teammate.

  • The name's own story, not another chat bubble
  • Each string is a starter teammate's colour; nothing else has colour
  • Small cut with a heavier frame keeps 16 px legible
  • Thin graphite frame, same weight as the Poppins Medium wordmark
